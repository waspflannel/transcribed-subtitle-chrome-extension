# Phase 08: Marketing And Growth

Status: planned
Owner: agent
Created: 2026-05-20
Last updated: 2026-05-20

## Goal

Create a measurable growth engine for the beta product without over-investing before retention and margins are proven.

Marketing should focus on polyglot power users who already watch foreign-language YouTube content and care about faster, better learning workflows.

## Scope

- In scope:
  - Positioning, messaging, launch channels, analytics, conversion funnel, and retention loops.
  - SEO/content experiments tied to language-learning and YouTube subtitle use cases.
  - Lightweight referral, creator, and community outreach experiments.
  - Beta case studies and testimonials where users consent.
- Out of scope:
  - Large paid ad spend before conversion and retention are proven.
  - Broad consumer branding exercise.
  - Enterprise sales.
  - Affiliate program automation unless manual experiments prove value.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Product positioning clearly targets polyglot power users and differentiates from normal YouTube captions.
- [ ] Website analytics track visitor to signup to checkout to extension install to first generation.
- [ ] SEO pages target concrete use cases, languages, and pain points without promising unsupported platforms.
- [ ] At least three growth channels are tested with measurable outcomes.
- [ ] Retention loops exist for low usage, near-limit usage, failed first job, and successful first generation.
- [ ] Marketing claims are backed by beta evidence for speed, quality, and supported workflow.

## Key Implementation Areas

- Positioning:
  - Core promise, user persona, before/after workflow, and differentiators.
  - Clear language around AI limits, provider quality, and public YouTube scope.
- Funnel analytics:
  - Landing visit, pricing view, signup, checkout start, purchase, extension install, login, first generation, first completed track, second session.
  - Keep generated content out of analytics payloads.
- Content:
  - Pages for "AI subtitles for YouTube language learning", language-specific use cases, WER accuracy caveats, and workflow guides.
  - Beta changelog and roadmap communication.
- Channels:
  - Polyglot communities, language-learning forums, YouTube learner communities, creator outreach, newsletter/waitlist, and targeted content.
- Retention:
  - Usage reminders, failed-job recovery, upgrade prompts near limits, and "try another language pair" prompts.

## Required Product Decisions

- Final brand and domain.
- Primary acquisition channels for beta.
- Analytics provider and consent posture.
- Whether referral credits are allowed during beta.
- Which proof points can be publicly claimed from beta evidence.
- Content cadence and owner.

## Validation/Evidence Required

- Funnel dashboard with working events.
- SEO crawl and page-index readiness.
- Channel experiment log with traffic, signup, conversion, cost, and qualitative notes.
- Retention metrics for beta users.
- Marketing copy review against actual product capability.

## Risks and Follow-up Debt

- Marketing too early can amplify support and infrastructure gaps.
- Unsupported platform requests may dominate if messaging is not clear.
- Analytics can become noisy unless event names and funnel definitions are stable.
- Growth experiments should not add product commitments without roadmap updates.
